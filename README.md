# JAFFAR-S-POWER-BI-PROJECT-

Blinkit Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI Project

📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes Blinkit's sales performance across different outlet types, outlet sizes, locations, and product categories. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key business metrics such as Total Sales, Number of Items Sold, Average Sales, and Customer Ratings, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and optimize retail operations.

🚨 Business Problem
Blinkit operates through multiple outlet formats and serves thousands of products across various locations. Management faces challenges in:
Identifying which outlet types generate the highest revenue.
Understanding customer purchasing patterns across product categories.
Evaluating the impact of outlet size and location on sales performance.
Monitoring product quality and customer satisfaction through ratings.
Optimizing inventory distribution across different store formats.
Improving decision-making through a centralized performance monitoring system.
Without a unified reporting solution, extracting actionable insights from large volumes of sales data becomes difficult and time-consuming.

🎯 Goal of the Dashboard
The primary objectives of this dashboard are:
Track overall business performance through key KPIs.
Analyze sales trends over time.
Compare performance across outlet types and locations.
Understand customer preferences by product category and fat content.
Evaluate outlet efficiency using sales, ratings, and item visibility metrics.
Support strategic decisions related to expansion, inventory planning, and marketing.

💡 Business Impact & Key Insights
📈 Revenue Growth Opportunities
Supermarket Type 1 contributes the largest share of sales and should be prioritized for expansion.
🏪 Store Format Optimization
Medium-sized outlets outperform other sizes, suggesting an ideal balance between operational cost and revenue generation.
🌍 Geographic Expansion Strategy
Tier 3 cities show the strongest sales performance, indicating untapped opportunities in emerging markets.
🛒 Product Portfolio Optimization
Household, Dairy, and Snack Food categories consistently generate strong sales and should receive greater inventory focus.
⭐ Customer Experience Monitoring
Average ratings remain close to 4, indicating generally positive customer satisfaction levels.
📦 Inventory Management
Visibility and sales metrics help identify product placement effectiveness and support stock optimization.
